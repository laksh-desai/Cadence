Mock EHR form

A stand-in for Office Ally so you can validate the Cadence extension's click-to-pick mapping and fill without touching a real EHR. Serve this over http (python -m http.server) or enable "Allow access to file URLs" for the extension, open it, click the extension icon, connect to Cadence, and map/fill. Fields deliberately use a mix of id, name, and no-attribute so the selector fallback gets exercised.
