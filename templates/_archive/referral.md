steps:
  - label: "Why you're referring — the main concern"
    example: "Referring for an orthopedic evaluation of persistent right knee pain that hasn't improved with PT."
  - label: "Relevant medical history"
    optional: true
    example: "Osteoarthritis; prior meniscus repair in 2019."
  - label: "Social history"
    optional: true
    example: "Works as a nurse on 12-hour shifts, on her feet all day."
  - label: "Your exam findings, tests, or imaging"
    example: "Persistent effusion, flexion limited to 100 degrees, MRI shows cartilage wear."
  - label: "How it's affecting the patient's daily life"
    optional: true
    example: "Unable to work a full shift or climb the stairs at home."
  - label: "What you want from the specialist — specific questions"
    example: "Requesting assessment for a possible injection or surgical options."
  - label: "Follow-up and communication plan"
    optional: true
    example: "Please send your recommendations; the patient will continue PT in the meantime."
---
Professional letter to a receiving specialist. Sections (include only what is relevant to the referral): Reason for Referral; Relevant Medical History; Social History; Clinical Evaluation Summary (exam findings, tests, imaging if stated); Impact on Patient's Life; Goals of Referral (specific questions/outcomes sought); Follow-Up and Communication. Concise and focused on the referral concern.
