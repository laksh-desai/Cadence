---
id: soap
name: SOAP Note
mode: omit
carry: false
steps:
  - label: "Subjective — what the patient reports (complaint and history)"
    example: "Right shoulder pain for 3 weeks, worse reaching overhead, no numbness or tingling."
  - label: "Objective — only what you measured (vitals, exam, test results)"
    optional: true
    example: "Shoulder flexion limited to 140 degrees, painful arc, abduction strength 4 out of 5."
  - label: "Assessment and plan for each problem"
    example: "Rotator cuff tendinopathy; start rotator cuff strengthening, reassess in 2 weeks."
---
Sections in order: Subjective (chief complaint + history of present illness as one paragraph; relevant past/social/family history only if mentioned); Objective (ONLY clinician-measured findings — vitals, exam findings, completed test results — as bullets, only those explicitly mentioned, no empty bullets); Assessment & Plan (each explicit problem/impression with a short plan). Patient-reported items go only in Subjective; do not infer diagnoses.
