---
id: soappt
name: SOAP-format PT Follow-Up
mode: require
carry: true
steps:
  - label: "Subjective — visit context, how they've done since last visit, HEP adherence, precautions"
    example: "Follow-up visit; reports less pain and better sleep; doing the home program daily; weight-bearing as tolerated."
  - label: "Vitals — blood pressure and heart rate"
    example: "BP 126 over 78, heart rate 70."
  - label: "Objective exam — range of motion, strength (MMT grades), palpation, gait, pain"
    example: "Left knee active range 5 to 110 degrees; quad 4 out of 5; mild swelling; gait steady with a cane; pain 3 out of 10."
  - label: "Treatments performed, each with CPT and minutes"
    example: "Manual therapy 10 min; ther ex 20 min; gait training 15 min; reviewed the home program."
  - label: "Goal progress — short- and long-term; say if any were met"
    example: "Short-term goal to climb 8 stairs is met; long-term goal of returning to hiking is in progress."
  - label: "Assessment and response to treatment (good, fair, or poor)"
    example: "Improving; residual weakness remains; skilled PT is medically necessary; response good."
  - label: "Plan"
    example: "Continue 2x per week and advance strengthening."
---
Sections in order: Subjective (visit type/context, chief complaint & course since last visit, adherence/response to HEP, precautions [carry forward]); Objective (Vitals BP/HR; Active ROM by region with measurements; Manual Muscle Testing with grades; Palpation/Observation; Gait; Pain rating/location); Treatment (Patient Education; Manual Therapy with CPT+minutes; Therapeutic Exercise with CPT+minutes; Gait Training with CPT+minutes; HEP) — include a treatment only if performed, each starting "Minutes: __"; Goals: Short-Term [carry forward, mark MET], Long-Term [carry forward, mark MET]; Assessment (status/progress, impairments, medical necessity, Response to Treatment Good/Fair/Poor); Plan.
