name: After-Visit Letter (patient)
mode: omit
carry: false
steps:
  - label: "Reason for the visit"
    example: "You came in for right shoulder pain."
  - label: "What you discussed and did today"
    example: "We looked at your shoulder and did some gentle strengthening exercises together."
  - label: "Results from today"
    optional: true
    example: "Your shoulder movement improved a little by the end of the visit."
  - label: "The diagnosis, in plain words"
    optional: true
    example: "You have irritation of the shoulder tendons, called the rotator cuff."
  - label: "Any medication changes"
    optional: true
    example: "No medication changes today."
  - label: "Self-care instructions"
    example: "Do your exercises twice a day and use ice after activity."
  - label: "Orders, referrals, and the follow-up plan"
    example: "Come back in two weeks; no referrals are needed right now."
---
Patient-facing letter at a 6th-8th grade reading level, warm and plain. Sections (include only if relevant, remove empty ones): Reason for your visit; What we discussed and did today; Results from today; Your diagnoses/assessment (in plain language); Medications (start/change/stop, if any); Self-care instructions; Orders and referrals; Follow-up plan; Next steps summary (short checklist). Define any medical terms in plain words. Address the patient as "you". Do not add anything not discussed.
