INITIAL EVALUATION — first full-intake visit, baseline capture. Require-mode; carry-forward OFF.
Write each item below as its OWN "## " section (never fold several under one heading), in this order. Completeness means every STATED fact appears — do NOT assert an unstated normal/negative for a section the therapist didn't address; omit it or flag it instead.

Chief Complaint — presenting problem and its effect on function.
Diagnoses — medical / PT diagnoses stated; include an ICD-10 code ONLY if the therapist explicitly assigned one, never assign one yourself.
Medications — list every medication and dose stated; do not drop any.
Allergies — drug allergies, or "no known drug allergies" if stated.
Referral & Relevant History — referring diagnosis; past medical history (every condition stated); precautions / contraindications; prior therapy for this problem; prior level of function.
Social History & Living Environment — residence type, stairs / steps to enter the home, available support, code status.
Objective Summary — 2-3 sentences MAXIMUM, high-level only; NEVER restate the full dictation or repeat details that belong in the sections below (a real generation dumped the whole dictation here — do not).
Fall Risk — history of falls, steadiness, Timed Up and Go if stated.
Musculoskeletal Assessment — lower- and upper-extremity range of motion and strength by region, including any joint changes stated.
Other Systems — integumentary, neuromuscular, cognition / communication, pain (only the systems actually addressed).
Cardiopulmonary — blood pressure, heart rate, and O2 at rest and with activity, if stated.
Functional Mobility — bed mobility, transfers, gait with device / assist / distance and any gait deviations, stairs, and community mobility status.
Coordination / Sensation / Edema — only if addressed today.
Assessment Summary — clinical impressions; clinical complexity and comorbidity burden; reason skilled services are needed; rehab potential; focus of care; patient participation and understanding.
Short-Term Goals — measurable, with a target timeframe.
Long-Term Goals — measurable, tied to the prior level of function.
Plan of Treatment — frequency (visits per week), duration (total weeks), intensity (minutes per session), certification period, anticipated discharge / transition plan, and patient goals.
