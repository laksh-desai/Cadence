RE-EVALUATION / PROGRESS REPORT — PT reassessment, required at least every 10 treatment visits. Require-mode; carry-forward ON.
Write each item below as its OWN "## " section (never fold several under one heading), in this order. Compare today's values with the start of care or the previous report wherever both are stated. Completeness means every STATED fact appears — never assert an unstated normal finding.

Reporting Period — visit number, reporting-period dates, and number of visits attended, as stated.
Reason for Reassessment — routine progress report, or the significant change that prompted a re-evaluation (new clinical findings, a fall, hospitalization, failure to respond to the plan), exactly as stated.
Interval History — the patient's report since the last evaluation: pain, falls, hospital stays, medication or health changes.
Diagnoses — medical and PT diagnoses stated; include an ICD-10 code ONLY if the therapist explicitly assigned one.
Precautions [carry forward] — fall risk, weight-bearing status, and other precautions still in effect.
Fall Risk — falls this period, steadiness, fear of falling.
Objective Measures — standardized tests done today with score, device, and the earlier value they are compared with.
Musculoskeletal Assessment — range of motion and strength by region, compared with earlier values; only regions addressed.
Cardiopulmonary — blood pressure, heart rate and O2 if stated.
Functional Mobility [carry forward] — bed mobility, transfers, gait with distance / device / assist level, stairs, updated to today and compared with the previous status.
Goal Status — every previous goal, one line each, marked MET, PROGRESSING or NOT MET with its current value; a goal not met gets the stated reason.
Assessment Summary — progress since the start of care, remaining impairments and functional limits, why skilled physical therapy is still required, rehab potential, and any significant change in condition.
Short-Term Goals [carry forward] — updated or new measurable goals with a target timeframe.
Long-Term Goals [carry forward] — updated or new measurable goals tied to prior level of function.
Plan of Treatment — frequency, duration, intensity, updated certification period, treatment focus, and the anticipated discharge plan.
