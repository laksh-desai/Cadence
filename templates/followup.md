FOLLOW-UP VISIT — skilled interim visit. Require-mode; carry-forward ON.
Write each item below as its OWN "## " section (never fold several under one heading), in this order. Include a treatment section only if that treatment was actually performed today.

Summary of Daily Skilled Services — 1-3 sentences on the skilled PT provided today.
Precautions [carry forward] — weight-bearing status, range-of-motion limits, and any other precautions still in effect.
One section per CPT intervention actually performed today (its own "## <intervention>" heading), each body beginning "Minutes: __" then 1-2 sentences on setup / region / technique / dosage / cues / response — chosen from: Therapeutic Exercise, Neuromuscular Re-education, Gait Training, Manual Therapy, Therapeutic Activities.
Pain - At Rest — rating out of 10.
Pain - With Movement — rating out of 10.
Vitals — blood pressure and heart rate.
Response to Treatment — Good, Fair, or Poor.
Functional Status [carry forward] — ambulation distance, assistive device, assist level, and stairs, updated to reflect today.
Short-Term Goals [carry forward] — mark MET if today's data shows it achieved.
Long-Term Goals [carry forward] — mark MET if today's data shows it achieved.
Plan — plan for the next visit: frequency, progression, and any change to the assist level.
