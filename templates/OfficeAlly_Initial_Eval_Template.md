# Office Ally custom SOAP layout — "Cadence Initial Eval"

**For:** the Office Ally team building SD Mobile PT's custom note layouts.
**Used for:** the first visit of every episode of care. Cadence note type: *Initial Evaluation*
(CPT 97161 / 97162 / 97163, chosen by the therapist).

## How Cadence fills it

- The Cadence Chrome extension fills boxes **by label**. A box whose label is **exactly** a name
  below receives that Cadence section and nothing else. Please use these labels as written.
- Every box is a **multi-line free-text box**, 2,000 characters (Office Ally's normal limit). A
  longer section continues into the next box of the same S/O/A/P part as "(cont.)".
- Please keep the standard **Objective Notes**, **Functional Status**, **Assessment** and
  **Plan Notes** boxes as overflow.
- Cadence saves a draft only (Apply). The therapist reviews and signs.
- Optional: one extra box per part labelled "Subjective (Cont)", "Objective (Cont)",
  "Assessment (Cont)", "Plan (Cont)" gives long evaluations extra room. Cadence uses boxes
  labelled "(Cont)" only for continued text.

## Boxes

| # | SOAP part | Box label (exact) | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subjective | **Chief Complaint** | Presenting problem and its effect on function |
| 2 | Subjective | **Diagnoses** | Medical and PT diagnoses, ICD-10 if the therapist assigned it |
| 3 | Subjective | **Medications** | Every medication with dose |
| 4 | Subjective | **Allergies** | Drug allergies or NKDA |
| 5 | Subjective | **Referral & Relevant History** | Referral, past medical history, precautions, prior therapy, prior level of function |
| 6 | Subjective | **Social History & Living Environment** | Home type, steps to enter, support, code status |
| 7 | Objective | **Objective Summary** | 2–3 sentence overview |
| 8 | Objective | **Fall Risk** | Falls history, steadiness, TUG |
| 9 | Objective | **Musculoskeletal Assessment** | ROM and strength by region |
| 10 | Objective | **Other Systems** | Skin, neuro, cognition, communication, pain (only what was examined) |
| 11 | Objective | **Cardiopulmonary** | BP, HR, O2 at rest and with activity |
| 12 | Objective | **Functional Mobility** | Bed mobility, transfers, gait (distance / device / assist), stairs |
| 13 | Objective | **Coordination / Sensation / Edema** | Only when examined |
| 14 | Assessment | **Assessment Summary** | Impressions, complexity, skilled need, rehab potential, participation |
| 15 | Plan | **Short-Term Goals** | Measurable goals with timeframe |
| 16 | Plan | **Long-Term Goals** | Measurable goals tied to prior level of function |
| 17 | Plan | **Plan of Treatment** | Frequency, duration, intensity, certification period, discharge plan |
| 18 | Plan | **Billing - CPT & Minutes** | Written by Cadence's code: evaluation code to confirm (97161/2/3), any treatment codes with minutes, total treatment time, ICD-10 to confirm |

**Note:** Cadence's other evaluation note ("Initial Evaluation — Updated Version") writes four large
blocks (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan). If the practice uses it, add four boxes labelled
exactly **Subjective**, **Objective**, **Assessment** and **Plan**.
