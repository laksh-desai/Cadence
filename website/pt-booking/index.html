Book a visit for a patient.

Use this page when you're scheduling on a patient's behalf — on the phone with them, or after a visit. Patients booking for themselves use Book a visit.

How to book for a patient

  1. Pick the day and time in the calendar.
  2. Enter the patient's name, phone and email — not yours — so the confirmation and reminders go to them.
  3. No email? Use info@sdmobilept.com and tell the patient the time yourself.
  4. Add the visit address if it isn't on file.

Keep it minimal

  • Booking details go only to the practice's Google Calendar. Enter name, contact and address — no diagnoses or medical details. Those belong in the chart.

Need a time that isn't offered?

  • Open Google Calendar signed in with the practice account and create the event directly.

Calendar not showing? Open the booking page.
