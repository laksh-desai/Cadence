YOUR OWN EXERCISE PICTURES
==========================

Put photos or AI-generated images in this folder to replace the built-in drawings.

1. Name each file after the exercise ID, plus a step number:

     neck-chin-tuck-1.jpg     (start position)
     neck-chin-tuck-2.jpg     (end position)

   Exercises with more steps can have more pictures, e.g. hand-tendon-glides-1.jpg ... -5.jpg.
   A single picture is fine too:  leg-ankle-circles.jpg

   Allowed types: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp
   Landscape pictures (about 4:3) look best.

2. Restart the app (double-click stop_server.bat, then start_server.bat).
   Exercises with pictures will show them instead of the drawing, on screen and in print.

The exercise IDs and ready-made AI prompts are listed in AI_image_prompts.md
(in the main folder). To go back to the drawing, delete or rename that exercise's pictures
and restart the app.
